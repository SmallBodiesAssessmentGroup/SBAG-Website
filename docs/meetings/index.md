# Upcoming Meetings

## Community Forum at DPS 58

The Small Bodies Assessment Group (SBAG) invites the small bodies community to take part in a community forum at DPS. The forum will feature a mix of short updates on mission status, observatories, and recent workshops, along with open mic time for community discussion about emerging issues, opportunities, and priorities. This is a chance to hear what is happening across the field and help shape SBAG’s evolving role as an independent, community-driven forum. Participation by Zoom is free; in-person attendance requires registration for the DPS meeting.

**Whether attending remotely or in person, please register [here](https://forms.gle/hod9s1uAbgCGGn5W9).**
